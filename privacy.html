Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Sprintr is a training app for sprinters. This page explains exactly what it stores, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It describes what the app actually does. If you find something here that does not match how the app behaves, the app is wrong and should be fixed.

What is stored

  • Your account. Your email address, and a password that is stored only as a hash, never as text anyone can read. You can also sign in with a one-time email link instead of a password.
  • Your training. Workouts, sprint times, lift weights, goals, availability, competition seasons, and app settings.
  • Still frames from your clips. A handful of pictures from each clip, the moments the app measured, plus a small preview image. The video itself is never uploaded and is never stored.
  • Your scores. The measurements and grades produced from each clip.

How your video is analysed

Form grading happens on your own device. The pose model runs inside your browser, reads the video off your phone, and produces the measurements there. The video is not uploaded anywhere in order to be graded.

The video file never leaves your phone. Once the measuring is done, the app keeps a few still frames from it: the touchdown, the peak of the knee lift, and so on, because those are the instants the scores were read at. Those stills are uploaded so your history has something to show. The clip itself is not, and there is no copy of it anywhere but your own camera roll.

The AI coach notes are the exception

There is an optional checkbox labelled "Also get AI coach notes". It is off by default. If you tick it, six still frames from your clip are sent through our server to Anthropic's API, which generates the written notes and sends them back.

Only those still frames leave your device, never the video file. Our server does not keep them; it passes them on and forwards the reply. What Anthropic does with data sent to its API is governed by Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, not this one. If you leave the box unticked, nothing about your clip is ever sent to Anthropic.

We do keep a count of how many AI analyses you have run each day, to enforce the daily limit. That is a number, not your data.

Where it is kept

Accounts, training data and still frames are stored with Supabase, on servers in the United States. Supabase is the hosting provider; they store the data on our behalf.

The app itself is served as static files from GitHub Pages, and the pose model is downloaded from Google's public model host. Neither receives your training data.

Who can see it

Only you. Every table enforces row-level security, which means the database itself refuses to return one athlete's rows to another athlete, regardless of what the app asks for. Your still frames are stored in a private bucket and are reachable only through short-lived links generated for you.

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. There is no analytics or tracking in this app.

How long it is kept

  • Still frames, scores and training data are kept until you delete them.
  • Video is not kept at all, so there is nothing to expire.
  • The app used to upload clips and delete them after 30 days. If you used it before 10 September 2026 you may still have clips stored; those are being deleted automatically 30 days after they were uploaded, and no new ones are being added.

Deleting your data

Every clip in the history has a Delete button, which removes its still frames and its scores immediately, and the video too, for older sessions that still have one.

To delete everything, open Settings and choose Delete Account. It asks twice: once to confirm, then to type the word DELETE, and then permanently removes your frames, your scores, your workouts, times, goals, settings and the account itself. There is no undo and no backup to restore from.

If that ever fails, or you cannot get into your account, email the address below and it will be done for you.

Young athletes

This app is built for high-school and club sprinters, so many users are under 18. If you are under 13, do not create an account without a parent or guardian setting it up with you.

Pictures of a young person are sensitive, which is a large part of why the video itself is never uploaded. What is stored is a few stills, and the recording stays on the athlete's own phone. If you are a parent or guardian and want to see what is stored for your athlete, or want it deleted, email the address below and it will be handled.

Changes

If what the app does with your data changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top will tell you when.
