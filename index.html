How to film it
Stand square to the lane Side-on, level with where you'll be running. Angled from the end and the joint angles read wrong.
Fill the frame with the athlete Closer is better. Too far and there are not enough pixels of you to measure a joint.
One runner in shot With two people overlapping, the tracking hops between them and the scores are a blend of both.
  • Pan to keep yourself in frame. Three seconds of running beats a long clip you cross in one.
  • Keep your feet in the picture. Cut off at the bottom edge and touchdown cannot be found.
  • Film at 1080p, not 4K. Fewer pixels means more frames a second, and frames are what get measured.
  • Don't film into the sun or across deep shade. A silhouette has no joints.
  • Don't include the blocks-set and the run in one long clip if you only care about one of them.
  • Don't hand the phone to someone who'll zoom mid-run. Zoom changes your size and splits the tracking.
