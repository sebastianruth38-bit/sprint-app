Checklist cues

  • Forward shin angle at first steps (drive phase)
  • Full hip/knee/ankle extension at toe-off
  • High knee drive, dorsiflexed foot (toe up)
  • Relaxed face/shoulders, elbows ~90°
  • Tall posture at top speed, minimal vertical bounce
  • Active/clawing foot strike under the hips
