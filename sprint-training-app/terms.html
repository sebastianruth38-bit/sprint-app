Using Sprint Lab means you accept what is on this page. It is short on purpose.

What this app is

Sprint Lab measures sprint mechanics from video you record, and keeps a log of your training. It is a measurement tool built by an athlete for athletes.

What it is not

It is not a coach, and it is not medical advice. The scores describe what the camera could see in a second or two of running. They cannot see pain, fatigue, injury history, or anything about how your body actually feels.

Do not change how you run, or train through something that hurts, because a number on this screen said so. Talk to your coach, and to a medical professional for anything injury-related. You train at your own risk.

How accurate the scores are

The measurements come from a pose model estimating where your joints are in a video. It gets things wrong, especially when you are far from the camera, filmed at an angle, in poor light, or in shot only briefly. The app refuses to grade a clip it cannot read, but a refusal is not the only way it can be wrong — it can also produce a confident number from a bad read.

Treat the scores as one more piece of information, next to a stopwatch and your coach's eyes. Not above them.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Tell us if you think someone else is using it.

Your clips

You keep ownership of everything you upload. By uploading, you allow the app to store and process your clips for the purpose of showing them back to you and grading them — nothing else.

Only upload video you have the right to upload. If other people are recognisable in a clip, make sure they are fine with it. Do not upload anything illegal, or anything that is not sprint footage.

Availability

This is a personal project running on free hosting. It may be slow, break, or go away. There is no guarantee it will keep working, and no guarantee your data will still be there tomorrow — keep your own copies of clips that matter to you. Video is deleted after 30 days by design.

Cost

The app is free. If that ever changes, you will be told before anything is charged.

Ending it

You can stop using the app whenever you like and ask for your data to be deleted — see the Privacy Policy. Accounts that are used to upload things that do not belong here may be removed.
